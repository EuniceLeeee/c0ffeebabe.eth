# Ready updates that change Family membership

The previous maintenance planner recognized only a pure addition without an explicit candidate file. A mixed addition/removal entered full candidate review even when every surviving Family had the same candidate snapshots and definitions at the same cutoff.

The production maintenance entry now treats addition, removal and mixed activation edits as changes to the Family set. It checks surviving partition definitions and, when supplied, the hash of the explicitly selected durable nominations. It retains those results and scans/attests only supplementary Families. A changed nomination, discovery definition or admission rule still uses candidate review. An operator expecting an activation edit may require it explicitly to prevent an unexpected full review.

A previously unfinished fixed-source review can finish the same input without repeating survivor admission. Its parent generation, source, verification range, input fingerprint, survivor definitions, exact nomination contents, membership and verified memos are checked. Survivors use the sealed parent outcomes. The immutable input journal preserves the work already completed on newly enabled Families. The completed-result and remaining counts for the new Families are logged separately. The normal production compiler builds and checks the Graph from the resulting verified rows.

For the oct29 selection, 134,586 candidates from 29 surviving Families match the sealed baseline, including 73,265 previous verified outcomes. The interrupted general review had saved 117,397 distinct outcomes. Of these, 13,188 were in the seven newly enabled Families (18 OneSwap, 10 PrecisionPool, 12,929 Kyber Elastic and 231 Compound cToken). The new-Family queue therefore had 12,091 unfinished candidates (49 Peapods, 7 PSV and 12,035 Algebra). Beyond the 73,086 outcomes completed during the proof review, 31,123 survivor candidates were processed unnecessarily. Candidate counts do not measure RPC calls or provider charges.

The production build and both maintenance/runner regression suites passed. A same-state production CLI case at block 26,222,895 removed Families and enabled EtherToken native redeem while keeping Algebra. Only the one supplied EtherToken nomination ran in the supplement. The Algebra outcome map was identical and its two Graph edges remained. The interruption regression also demonstrated that the next strict call was only the outstanding candidate from the new Family. Starting policy is four candidate workers and up to eight RPC items per batch; transport throttling can reduce it.
