### A. Latency root-cause fix — ✅ FIXED (per-opp time budget; TTL band-aid reverted)
- **Symptom:** opps die `expired-before-solver` (killed our one live v4 opp in R6).
- **What the measurements actually show (two dry-run windows, TTL 5000 then 8000):** the expired opps' measured stages `match+fork+prep+detect+plan` sum to only **~130–250ms**, but `total` = the full TTL (5003ms at TTL5000; **8022–8495ms at TTL8000**). i.e. the **~5–8s is DEAD TIME not captured by any segMark** — and **`plan` is NOT it** (expired opps have `plan=8–25ms`). **Bumping the TTL did NOT fix it — the dead time just grew with the TTL** (still 3 expired at TTL8000, `solverEntered=52`, `simSuccess=0`).
- **Most likely root cause (verify first):** the **per-hint TTL is SHARED across a serial multi-opp loop** (`main.ts:1112` `for (const opp of opportunities)`; `startedAt=tHint` set once per hint at `main.ts:738`). The cost is the **SOLVER**, not the planner: `SEARCHER_SOLVER_DEADLINE_MS=8000` (`main.ts:300`) lets each opp's `solver.solve` burn up to 8s, and `simSuccess=0` means it runs to the full deadline finding nothing. So opp #1's solve eats the shared budget and opp #2/#3 expire with tiny individual stages. (The `plan=2.8–4.8s` tail seen on a few opps is a *secondary*, separate cost — real but not what killed the measured expired opps.)
- **Correcting the earlier hypothesis:** "planner time budget" is NOT the fix — the planner is bounded (`maxPoolsPerToken=8`, `maxHops=3` → ≤512 paths) and `plan` was fast on every expired opp. Do not chase the planner first.
- **First step — instrument the DEAD TIME (it's currently unmeasured):** `segMark` covers `fork/match/prep/detect/plan` but the gap between `plan` (`main.ts:1142`) and the per-candidate TTL check (`main.ts:1324`) — i.e. `victimApply` (1218), `prepareVictimState`/`overlay` (1261/1262), and **the per-opp `solve` (1350)** — never shows in the expired `segStr`. Add timing there + log the **opportunities-per-hint count** to confirm the multi-opp-starvation theory.
- **Likely fixes (once confirmed):** (1) **give each opportunity its own time budget** instead of one shared per-hint TTL (reset the clock per opp, or divide the budget); (2) **shorten `SEARCHER_SOLVER_DEADLINE_MS`** (8s is huge — a solve that will `simSuccess=0` should bail fast); (3) **early-exit the solver** on a cheap no-profit quote before full GSS/sim; (4) optionally cap opportunities processed per hint.
